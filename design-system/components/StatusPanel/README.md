# StatusPanel

What the Partner portal shows instead of the menu until a partner is active: the request's status, a line, and the next step.

- **You supply** `status`, `title`, `text` (the rejection reason) and `action`. Texts: "Your partner account is active." · "Your request is under review." · rejected with the reason and Edit and send again · "Your account has been deactivated. Please contact the administrator for further assistance." Source: PK-P2.
