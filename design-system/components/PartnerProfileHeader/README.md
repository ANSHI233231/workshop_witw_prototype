# PartnerProfileHeader

The top of a Partner Profile: logo, business name, About your business, and count tiles that open their tab.

- Provide: `name`, `counts` `[{label,value}]` (Packages, Parks); optional `logo`, `initials`, `about`, `active`, `onTile(index)`.
- The partner's phone and email are not shown. An inactive partner's link goes to the home page.
