# PartnerProfileHeader

The top of a Partner Profile: the 88px logo (or white initials on `primary`), the business name, About your business, and the Packages and Parks count tiles.

- Provide: `name`, `counts` `[{label,value}]`; optional `logo`, `initials`, `about`, `active`, `onTile(index)` (a tile opens its tab).
- The current tile gets a `primary` ring. Phone and email are never shown. An inactive partner's link goes to the home page.
- Source: U2 Partner Profile.
