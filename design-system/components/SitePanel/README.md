# SitePanel

A white panel with a green title bar, used down the safari page: Safari Plan, Discussion, Interested.

- **You supply** `title` and the body: `text` for plain text (line breaks kept), `faces` for a row of people, or any `children` (Comment…). `note` adds a muted line at the bottom ("Join this safari to comment.").
- With `onTitle` the title bar becomes a button (Interested opens the list of who joined); give it an `icon`.
- `radius-lg` corners, `border` outline; title 16px semibold white on `primary`.
- Source: W3, W4.
